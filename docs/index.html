      About

      IndusScript AI investigates the undeciphered Indus Valley writing system with computational linguistics, historical Meluhha/trade evidence, comparative statistics, and falsification controls. The full Python/Streamlit research platform lives in the GitHub repository. This GitHub Pages site is a public, interactive speculative demo anyone can use in the browser.

      Integrity: Visual similarity and structural scores are not proof of common ancestry or shared meaning. Candidate readings are hypotheses until independently supported. Meluhha in Mesopotamian texts is not automatically the language of Indus inscriptions.

      Repository: github.com/AmmarJamshed/indusscript-ai

      Four research questions (kept distinct)

      1. A. Are the symbols visually similar?
      2. B. Do the writing systems exhibit similar structural properties?
      3. C. Is there evidence of linguistic relationships, borrowing or contact?
      4. D. Can any Indus sign sequences be assigned defensible candidate meanings?
      Public demo for education and research triage. Not a claim that Indus has been deciphered.
