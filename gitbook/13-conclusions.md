# Conclusions & Roadmap

## Conclusions

IndusScript AI operationalises a **responsible computational agenda** for Indus research: multi-corpus comparison under epistemic controls, historical contact indexing without language conflation, and mandatory falsification before interpretive excitement.

The platform’s public Speculative Translation Lab shows how universities can invite public interaction **without manufacturing decipherment theatre**.

## Near-term roadmap

1. Licence-cleared selective imports from CDLI / ORACC / TLA / HF datasets
2. Curator-approved Indus signary images replacing geometric proxies
3. Optional Transformer masked-sign models behind the same evaluation harness
4. Shared course packs (assignments + grading rubrics)
5. Multi-institution sign-off on evidence-tier guidelines

## Closing line for examiners

> The measure of success is not a viral translation. It is whether a claim survives provenance checks, question separation, and null models — and whether students learn to demand the same.
