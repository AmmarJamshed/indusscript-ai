# References

## Methodological & historical touchstones (selective)

Scholars and projects commonly consulted when designing comparative Indus workflows include (non-exhaustive; cite primary editions in theses):

* Parpola, Asko — Indus script surveys and sign lists
* Mahadevan, Iravatham — concordance traditions
* Farmer, Sproat, Witzel — debates on linguistic vs non-linguistic status
* CDLI / ORACC / ETCSL documentation for Mesopotamian corpora
* Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae (TLA) for Egyptian annotations
* Scholarship on Meluhha, Dilmun, and Magan in third-millennium trade

This white paper is a **software and methods** document. It is not a substitute for philological editions. Replace demonstration citations with peer-reviewed primary publications when publishing empirical claims.
